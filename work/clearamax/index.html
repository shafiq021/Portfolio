A Pakistani WooCommerce store for bulk and B2B buyers. I audited the site and delivered a fix list.

Context

Clearamax is a WooCommerce store selling in bulk to business buyers, priced in PKR and still in development. The owner asked for an audit before launch.

Idea

Audit first, fix in priority order. A store that launches with a broken homepage title, a missing H1 and a form that mislabels phone numbers loses the sale before the product gets a chance.

Craft

The audit found seven critical issues: no H1 on the homepage, a homepage title that was only the domain, one testimonial attributed to two reviewers, a plugin hijacking the tab title, a WhatsApp bubble covering the mobile menu icon, forms named "New Form" with number type phone fields, and no favicon.

Outcome

Fix list delivered to the owner, in priority order, with the store work to follow.
